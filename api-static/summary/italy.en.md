# Italy Deepfake Law Summary

## Core Legal Framework
- Law No. 132 of 23 September 2025 is in force from 10 October 2025 and provides Italy's central AI-law framework in this corpus. (Ref1)
- Article 26(c) inserts Penal Code Article 612-quater, directly criminalizing harmful dissemination without consent of AI-falsified or altered images, video, or voices capable of misleading viewers as to authenticity. (Ref1)

## Summary
- Italy combines general principles for responsible and transparent AI use with a direct synthetic-media criminal offence. (Ref1)
- Article 612-quater requires unauthorized dissemination, deceptive authenticity, and unjust harm. It carries imprisonment from one to five years. Prosecution is complaint-based, subject to listed ex officio exceptions. (Ref1)
- The reviewed law does not create an election-specific deepfake rule or a dedicated platform notice-and-removal duty. Its general transparency principles are not equivalent to a universal public-content label mandate. (Ref1)

## Policy Dimensions
- `Labeling status`: partly codified through general transparency principles, but no dedicated general deepfake label or watermark rule was identified. (Ref1) Relevant text: pp. 1-3, Arts. 1-4.
- `Election-related status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. No election-specific synthetic-media rule was identified in Law No. 132/2025. (Ref1) Relevant text: reviewed Law No. 132/2025.
- `Non-consensual deepfake status`: legislated. Penal Code Art. 612-quater directly covers harmful dissemination without consent of AI-falsified or altered images, video, or voices that can mislead as to authenticity. (Ref1) Relevant text: p. 17, Art. 26(c), inserting Art. 612-quater.
- `Service-provider/platform liability status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. The reviewed provision criminalizes conduct but does not establish a deepfake-specific platform duty. (Ref1) Relevant text: p. 17, Art. 612-quater.
- `Penalty/sanctions codification status`: legislated. Article 612-quater provides imprisonment from one to five years, with complaint-based prosecution and listed ex officio exceptions. (Ref1) Relevant text: p. 17, Art. 612-quater.

Ref1: `sources/official/italy/1_official-law-act/italy-2025-artificial-intelligence-law-no-132-it.pdf`

## Practice Notes (by Dimension)
- `Labeling`: distinguish general AI transparency principles from content-level disclosure mandates.
- `Election`: do not infer election regulation from the general AI law.
- `Non-consensual deepfakes`: test consent, deceptive authenticity, dissemination, and unjust harm as separate elements.
- `Service-provider/platform liability`: keep offender liability separate from intermediary duties.
- `Penalty`: cite the one-to-five-year range together with the complaint/ex officio procedural distinction.

## Verify Before Use
- Check later implementing or election-specific measures before claiming the current framework is exhaustive.
- Use the official Italian text as controlling; translated descriptions are reading aids.
