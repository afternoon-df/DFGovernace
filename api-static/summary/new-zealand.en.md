# New Zealand Deepfake Law Summary

## Core Legal Framework
- The Deepfake Digital Harm and Exploitation Bill 213-1 was introduced on 23 October 2025 and remains proposal-stage in the current corpus. (Ref1)
- The bill would amend the Crimes Act and Harmful Digital Communications Act definitions so created, synthesised, or altered intimate depictions are covered by existing offences and remedies. (Ref1)

## Summary
- New Zealand's reviewed proposal directly addresses synthetic intimate imagery rather than relying only on general impersonation or privacy concepts. (Ref1)
- Proposed Crimes Act s. 216G(1A) covers created, synthesised, or altered intimate depictions made without the depicted person's knowledge or consent. A parallel Harmful Digital Communications Act amendment would extend the remedial definition. (Ref1)
- The source is not enacted law, and the bill does not add a general labeling, election, or new platform-duty regime. Exact penalties require cross-reference to the Acts being amended. (Ref1)

## Policy Dimensions
- `Labeling status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. The bill does not create a general synthetic-media disclosure or watermarking mandate. (Ref1) Relevant text: pp. 1-4.
- `Election-related status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. No election-specific rule was identified. (Ref1) Relevant text: pp. 1-4.
- `Non-consensual deepfake status`: proposed direct coverage. The bill expressly includes created, synthesised, or altered intimate depictions made without knowledge or consent. (Ref1) Relevant text: p. 4, proposed Crimes Act s. 216G(1A).
- `Service-provider/platform liability status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. The bill extends existing Harmful Digital Communications Act remedies but does not establish a new general deepfake platform-duty clause. (Ref1) Relevant text: p. 4, proposed definition amendment.
- `Penalty/sanctions codification status`: proposed extension of existing offences and remedies. Exact imprisonment or fine values are not stated in the reviewed amendment text and require cross-reference. (Ref1) Relevant text: pp. 1-2 explanatory note and p. 4 amendments.

Ref1: `sources/official/new-zealand/2_draft/new-zealand-2025-deepfake-digital-harm-exploitation-bill-213-1-en.pdf`

## Practice Notes (by Dimension)
- `Labeling`: do not infer a general label rule from intimate-image definition amendments.
- `Election`: election regulation remains unconfirmed in the current corpus.
- `Non-consensual deepfakes`: the synthetic wording is direct, but the status remains Proposed.
- `Service-provider/platform liability`: distinguish extension of existing remedies from creation of a new proactive duty.
- `Penalty`: verify the current Crimes Act penalty provision before stating a numeric sentence.
