# Malaysia Deepfake Law Summary

## Core Legal Framework
- Malaysia is represented by the official Cyber Crime Bill D.R. 8/2026, first read on 22 June 2026. It is proposal-stage material, not enacted law. (Ref1)
- Full-text review found no express deepfake, synthetic-media labeling, election-deepfake, or synthetic intimate-image provision in this bill. (Ref1)

## Summary
- The current source updates Malaysia from “collection pending” to “official bill reviewed,” but it does not establish a confirmed deepfake-specific legal pathway. (Ref1)
- General cybercrime provisions should not be converted into deepfake regulation without a direct article-level anchor. (Ref1)
- Malaysia therefore remains a no-confirmed-law case across the five deepfake policy dimensions in this corpus, while the bill itself remains visible as proposed cybercrime context. (Ref1)

## Policy Dimensions
- `Labeling status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. The reviewed bill contains no confirmed synthetic-media disclosure, watermarking, or labeling mandate. (Ref1) Relevant text: full-text keyword and clause review of D.R. 8/2026.
- `Election-related status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. No election-specific deepfake clause was identified. (Ref1) Relevant text: full-text review of D.R. 8/2026.
- `Non-consensual deepfake status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. No express synthetic intimate-image provision was identified. (Ref1) Relevant text: full-text review of D.R. 8/2026.
- `Service-provider/platform liability status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. General cybercrime provisions do not by themselves establish a deepfake-specific platform-duty regime. (Ref1) Relevant text: full-text review of D.R. 8/2026.
- `Penalty/sanctions codification status`: no dedicated law in the current corpus. No deepfake-specific offence or sanction was confirmed in the reviewed bill. (Ref1) Relevant text: full-text review of D.R. 8/2026.

Ref1: `sources/official/malaysia/2_draft/malaysia-2026-cyber-crime-bill-dr-8-2026-ms.pdf`

## Practice Notes (by Dimension)
- `Labeling`: keep labeling at No Law until an express official clause is added.
- `Election`: do not infer election coverage from the cybercrime title.
- `Non-consensual deepfakes`: require explicit synthetic or altered-image wording before upgrading the status.
- `Service-provider/platform liability`: separate general cybercrime administration from content-specific intermediary duties.
- `Penalty`: do not add a deepfake penalty row without a qualifying offence provision.

## Verify Before Use
- Check later parliamentary versions or enacted text for amendments introducing direct synthetic-media language.
- Use the official Malay bill as controlling.
