# Tanzania Deepfake Law Summary

## Core Legal Framework
- Tanzania is represented by the Cybercrimes Act No. 14 of 2015, effective 1 September 2015, in an official revised edition. (Ref1)
- Sections 15 and 16 provide in-force computer impersonation and false/deceptive/misleading information offences. They are adjacent to some deepfake harms but do not expressly mention deepfakes or synthetic media. (Ref1)

## Summary
- Section 15 criminalizes computer-system impersonation and provides a minimum TZS 5 million fine or three times the undue advantage, imprisonment for at least seven years, or both. (Ref1)
- Section 16 criminalizes knowing publication of false, deceptive, misleading, or inaccurate computer information with listed harmful intent and provides a minimum TZS 5 million fine, imprisonment for at least three years, or both. (Ref1)
- These offences may be relevant to impersonation or deceptive synthetic content, but the present corpus does not justify treating them as dedicated deepfake legislation. (Ref1)

## Practice Notes (by Dimension)

- `Service-provider/platform liability`: do not infer proactive intermediary duties from offender provisions.
- `Penalty`: identify ss. 15-16 as adjacent cybercrime penalties, not deepfake-specific sentencing.
